In voice mode, scrolling starts when you start reading and pauses whenever you stop or go off-script. Drag the script to any point and start reading from there — tracking re-anchors to the line at the arrow. Perfect takes grades each sentence — stumbles, filler words, or mid-sentence pauses send you back to retake the line until it's clean (deliberate pauses at commas and sentence ends are always fine). Pinch with two fingers to resize the text.
